Support Our Work

The Reformed Hope Foundation exists so that children, families and adults with disabilities in Northern Virginia have somewhere to turn. Giving to the Foundation helps sustain that work — the support group where caregivers find each other, the workshops built for learners with differing abilities, and the practical help that lets families keep going.

Ways to give

Online giving is on its way

We are setting up a secure donation platform. Until it is live, you can reach the Foundation directly and we will arrange your gift with you.

Secure online donations coming soon

The Foundation is finalizing its donation platform. This space is reserved for secure online giving.

Contact us about donating

Prefer to help another way? You can also join Caring for Caregivers and share the group with someone who needs it, or read more about the Foundation before you give.

In the meantime

There is another way to be part of this.

Caring for Caregivers meets bimonthly for community, information sharing and support. If you are caring for someone, you are welcome there.
