Care Works

Concierge services for caregivers.

Find trustworthy support for yourself as you care for others. We start with a free consultation, then build a care plan around what you actually need.

How it works

A care plan shaped around your household

Every caregiver’s situation is different, so Care Works begins with a free consultation rather than a fixed package.

From that conversation we put together a customized care plan built around your needs, and quote the services you decide to use.

Services

What Care Works can take off your plate

  • Personal Shopping

    Errands and shopping handled for you, so the list stops growing.

  • Lifestyle Scheduling and Planning

    Appointments, routines and commitments organized into a schedule that holds together.

  • Meal Planning

    Meals planned around your household’s needs, preferences and week.

  • Caregiver Resourcing

    Help finding support — the services, programs and people you need but have not had time to track down.

  • Custom Care Packages

    A combination of the above, assembled to fit your care plan.
