Privacy

What this website does and does not collect.

The short version: this site does not track you. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from anyone else’s servers.

This website

Nothing is collected about your visit

There is no analytics software on this site — no Google Analytics, no tag manager, no advertising or social media pixel, no heatmap or session recording. No cookies are set. Nothing about your visit is stored in your browser.

The site also makes no requests to third-party servers. Its typeface, stylesheet, images and the one small script that opens the mobile menu are all served from this domain, so simply reading a page here does not reveal your visit to any other company.

Like every website, the service that hosts these pages keeps standard server logs, which typically include IP addresses and are retained for a short period for security and reliability. The Foundation does not use those logs to build any profile of visitors.

When you contact us

Services run by other companies

Some things the Foundation offers are hosted elsewhere. When you follow one of those links you leave this site, and that company’s privacy policy applies to what you do there.

  • The contact form

    Our contact and registration form is a Google Form. What you submit goes to Google and is visible to the Foundation. We use it only to reply to you and to run the program you asked about, and we do not sell, rent or share it.

  • Caring for Caregivers on Meetup

    The support group is organized on Meetup. Joining requires a Meetup account, and your membership of the group is governed by Meetup’s own terms and privacy policy.

  • Donations

    Online giving is not yet live. When it is, donations will be handled by a dedicated payment provider. The Foundation will never collect card or bank details directly through this website.

Young people

Information about children

Financial Literacy for Youth serves children aged 8 to 12. Registration is completed by a parent or guardian, and the Foundation asks only for what it needs to run the program safely. Information about a child is never used for anything else, and a parent or guardian may ask us to delete it at any time.

Questions

Asking about your information

If you would like to know what the Foundation holds about you, or want it corrected or deleted, please get in touch and we will help.
