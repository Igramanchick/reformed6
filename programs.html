Programs

Support for caregivers. Skills for young learners.

The Reformed Hope Foundation runs two programs: a bimonthly support group for caregivers, and a quarterly financial literacy program for youth ages 8–12, designed for learners with differing abilities.

Bimonthly support group

Caring for Caregivers

A bimonthly support group for caregivers to experience community, engage in information sharing and receive support.

Caring for someone with a disability can be isolating work. This group exists so that it does not have to be.

Sign up for Caring for Caregivers on Meetup

Meetings are organized through the Foundation’s Meetup group, where you will find upcoming dates and details.

Quarterly · Ages 8–12

Financial Literacy for Youth

A quarterly initiative to train youth ages 8–12 in responsible financial management skills and raise awareness about investment opportunities.

The goal

To help young people ages 8–12 make informed decisions about managing money and set longer-term financial goals, through workshops adapted for learners with differing abilities.

Sample workshops

Workshop content is tailored by age group. These are representative of what a session covers.

  1. Income and Savings Plans

    • Budget planning
    • Weekly spending tracker
    • Tracking income and debt
    • Savings planner

  2. Debt Reduction and Asset Building

    • What is debt
    • What is an asset
    • Credit cards and student loans as debts
    • Home ownership and education as assets
    • Retirement planning

  3. Credit Rating

    • What a credit score is and why it matters
    • The Fair Credit Reporting Act
    • Reading, fixing and maintaining a credit report
