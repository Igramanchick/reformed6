About

A place for families to connect and build community.

The Reformed Hope Foundation provides educational and social support to children, families and adults with disabilities in Northern Virginia.

What drives us

Mission and vision

Our mission

To provide educational and social support to children, families and adults with disabilities in Northern Virginia. We believe that every child needs a solid educational foundation, and every adult with a disability deserves a chance to live with dignity in their community.

Our vision

We envision a world where children and people living with disabilities are treated fairly, with care, and that families have the supports they need to fulfill their dreams.

Leadership

Who leads this work

Allison E. Viescas

Allison leads the Reformed Hope Foundation’s work across Northern Virginia, from the Caring for Caregivers support group to the Foundation’s financial literacy programming for young learners with differing abilities.
