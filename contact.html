Get in touch with the Foundation.

Whether you are a caregiver looking for support, a family interested in a program, or an organization exploring a partnership, here is the right way to reach us.

How to reach us

Pick the reason that fits

Every route below reaches the same small team. Choosing the closest match just helps us answer you faster.

  • General questions and partnerships

    Questions about the Foundation, ideas for a partnership, or interest in volunteering. Send the Foundation a message.

  • Caring for Caregivers

    The bimonthly support group is organized on Meetup, where you will find upcoming dates and can join the group. Caring for Caregivers on Meetup.

  • Financial Literacy for Youth

    To register a young person aged 8–12 for the quarterly program, or to ask about upcoming sessions, get in touch about Financial Literacy for Youth. See the program details first if you have not already.

  • Care Works services

    To arrange a free consultation about care coordination, respite or the Foundation’s other services, request a Care Works consultation. What Care Works covers.

  • Giving and donations

    Questions about supporting the Foundation’s work, including gifts by other means. Support our work.

The Foundation is a small organization run largely by volunteers, so please allow a few days for a reply.

Your information

What happens to what you send

This website itself collects nothing about you — there are no analytics, no cookies and no tracking of any kind. The contact form and the support-group signup are hosted by Google and Meetup respectively, so anything you submit there is handled under those companies’ terms. Read the full privacy note.
